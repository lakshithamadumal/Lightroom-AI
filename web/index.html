How Lightroom AI Studio Works

v1.0.6

Comprehensive Setup & 100% Adobe Lightroom Engine Guide

1

OrcaRouter Vision AI Setup

To enable AI-assisted composition analysis and dynamic auto-balancing, you need an OrcaRouter API token.

Sign Up with GitHub: Visit orcarouter.ai/console/token and sign in with GitHub.
Generate Token: Create your personal access token and copy the sk-orca-... key.
Save in Studio Settings: Click Settings (⚙️) in the top-right header, paste your key in the OrcaRouter API Key field, and click Save Settings.
2

100% Exact Adobe Lightroom Preset (3D HALD LUT)

Our engine uses 3D HALD color mapping to achieve 100% exact mathematical match with Adobe Lightroom develop profiles without any color drift.

Neutral HALD LUT Base Image 512×512 PNG • Level 8 Identity 3D Color Matrix
Download LUT Base
A. Open Adobe Lightroom Classic or Lightroom CC on your computer.
B. Import the downloaded neutral_lut.png image.
C. Apply your desired Lightroom Preset (Tone Curves, HSL colors, Shadows, Highlights, Vibrance).
D. Export the image as JPG or PNG (100% Quality, sRGB, 512×512).
E. Place the exported LUT in C:/Media_Presets or select it directly via "Pick File" in Settings!
3

Batch Automation & Live Streaming

Drop your unedited raw photos in C:/Media_Incoming, choose your AI Crop and Auto-Balancing toggles in Settings, and click "Start" in the top capsule.
